###  Admin Dashboard Features

#### 1. Overview
- **Dashboard:** Real-time statistical overview of total volunteers, pending applications, active events, and recent activities.
- **Applications:** 
  - View all incoming NSS volunteer applications.
  - Filter by status (Pending, Approved, Rejected).
  - Review detailed application forms with documents.
  - **Action:** Approve or Reject applications with custom remarks. Automated emails are sent upon status change.

#### 2. People & Access
- **User Management:** 
  - Manage system administrators and sub-admins.
  - Assign specific roles and permissions (RBAC) to control access to different parts of the system.
- **Volunteers:** 
  - Comprehensive list of all active volunteers.
  - View detailed volunteer profiles, including attendance and points.
  - Manage volunteer status and disciplinary actions.

#### 3. Programs & Events
- **Projects:** 
  - Create and manage long-term NSS projects (e.g., "Clean India", "Literacy Drive").
  - Assign volunteers to specific projects.
  - Track project progress and impact.
- **Events:** 
  - Schedule and publish upcoming events.
  - Set event details: date, time, location, and points awarded.
  - Manage event registrations.
- **Attendance Review:** 
  - Verify volunteer attendance for events.
  - Validate location-based selfies or proofs uploaded by volunteers.
  - Approve or reject attendance claims to award hours/points.
- **Meetings:** 
  - Schedule official NSS meetings.
  - Track meeting attendance and minutes.

#### 4. Engagement & Support
- **Announcements:** 
  - Broadcast important messages to all or specific groups of volunteers.
  - Announcements appear on volunteer dashboards.
- **Social Media Posts:** 
  - Manage and curate content for social media.
  - Track engagement metrics (if integrated).
- **Grievances:** 
  - A dedicated portal to receive and resolve complaints or concerns raised by volunteers.
  - Track grievance status from "Open" to "Resolved".
- **Reports & Issues:** 
  - View system reports and operational issues.
  - Generate PDF reports for official documentation.

#### 5. System Settings
- **NSS Social Links:** Configure the official social media links displayed on the public portal.
- **Email System:** 
  - Configure email templates for notifications.
  - View email logs to ensure delivery.
  - Send bulk emails to volunteers.
- **Volunteer Password Reset:** Administrative tool to reset passwords for volunteers who cannot access their accounts.

### Volunteer Dashboard Features

#### 1. Core Activities
- **Dashboard:** Personal overview showing total hours worked, events attended, and upcoming tasks.
- **NSS Projects:** 
  - Browse available projects.
  - Join projects of interest.
  - View updates on joined projects.
- **Events:** 
  - Calendar view of upcoming NSS events.
  - **Action:** One-click registration for events.
  - Mark attendance by uploading required proofs (e.g., geo-tagged photos).
- **Tasks:** 
  - View specific tasks assigned by admins.
  - Update task status (In Progress, Completed).

#### 2. Profile & Identity
- **Complete Profile:** 
  - Manage personal details, academic info, and skills.
  - Ensure profile is 100% complete to unlock all features.
- **My Application:** View the original NSS application and its status history.
- **ID Card:** 
  - **Feature:** Auto-generated digital NSS Identity Card.
  - Downloadable PDF format for printing or official use.

#### 3. Support & Feedback
- **Grievances:** 
  - Submit complaints or suggestions directly to the administration.
  - Track the status of submitted grievances.
- **Reports & Issues:** Report technical issues or submit activity reports.

#### 4. Account
- **Settings:** Manage account preferences and notification settings.
- **Change Password:** Securely update login credentials.
